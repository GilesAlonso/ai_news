Published on 2026-08-06

World

  • Iran–Oman proposal would give Tehran control over inbound traffic through the Strait of Hormuz
    Sources told Reuters on August 5 that a proposed arrangement could give Iran control over ships entering the Gulf through the strait. Washington has said a reopening agreement is imminent, while key details remain unresolved.
  • Iran warns Gulf states of retaliation if the United States launches further strikes
    Five sources told Reuters that Tehran warned Gulf states that additional U.S. attacks on Iranian territory could lead to retaliation against regional energy infrastructure.
  • Germany opens a counterterrorism investigation after an explosive drone is found near Leipzig airport
    German authorities said an explosive-laden drone was found near a runway on August 5. Parts of the cargo hub were closed while investigators examined the device and a related aircraft incident.

USA

  • Trump says he has not decided whether to remove U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro
    Trump said on August 5 that he had not made a decision about Pirro's position after her office dropped charges against an Olympic canoeist in the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool case.
  • Abdul El-Sayed wins the Democratic Senate nomination in Michigan
    El-Sayed defeated Representative Haley Stevens in the August 4 Democratic primary, according to the Associated Press. The result is being treated as a test of the party's policy direction ahead of the November election.
  • New Mexico sues the federal government for access to Epstein-related files
    New Mexico officials filed a lawsuit seeking access to records connected to Jeffrey Epstein. The state's investigator cited questions involving evidence, jurisdiction and the passage of time.

Brazil

  • Flávio Bolsonaro names Alfredo Gaspar as his vice-presidential running mate
    The senator and presidential candidate announced Congressman Alfredo Gaspar on August 5 for the October election. Reuters reported that the choice followed difficulty building alliances beyond the Liberal Party.
  • Brazil accuses the Trump and Milei administrations of attempting election interference
    Brazil's government made the accusation on August 5, according to UPI. The report did not establish that the alleged interference occurred or describe a court finding.
  • Brazil's presidential contest enters a more defined October phase
    The announcement of the Bolsonaro ticket gives voters a clearer opposition slate to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva. The competing campaigns are expected to focus on alliances, economic policy and the role of foreign governments.
